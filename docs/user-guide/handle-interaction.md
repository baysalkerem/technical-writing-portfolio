# Handle a chat interaction

Accept an incoming chat, reply to the customer, and record the outcome.

## Prerequisites

- [Set your status to Available](./manage-availability.md#set-your-status-to-available) and confirm your chat queue shows **Ready**.

## Accept an interaction

1. In the incoming interaction notification, confirm the channel is **Chat** and check the queue name.
2. Select **Accept**. The conversation opens in the interaction panel.
3. Review the customer details and available interaction history.
4. Type your reply in **Message**.
5. Select **Send**. Your reply appears in the conversation.

If the offer closes before you accept it, stay **Available** for the next offer. If you expected another offer and it does not arrive, see [If you are not receiving offers](./manage-availability.md#if-you-are-not-receiving-offers).

## Complete the interaction

1. Select **End interaction** when the conversation finishes.
2. In the wrap-up panel, select an outcome code.
3. Enter a short summary of what you did and the result. Exclude passwords, payment card details, and other sensitive information.

   Example: Helped the customer reset their password and confirmed they could sign in.

4. Select **Save and close**.
5. In **Activity history**, open the saved interaction and confirm it shows the outcome code and summary.

If the workspace reports a save error, keep the wrap-up panel open and retry. If the error persists, give your supervisor the interaction ID and the error message.
