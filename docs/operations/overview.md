# Operate Nexus Connect

<PortfolioNotice />

Use this guide to verify interaction delivery during an upgrade and investigate issues with agent sign-in, routing, CRM data, or disconnected interactions.

## Operations tasks

- [Upgrade a deployment](./upgrade-workflow.md) to compare interaction delivery before and after a change, and decide whether to continue or escalate.
- [Troubleshoot common issues](./troubleshooting.md) to narrow down a symptom and identify the team that can resolve it.
- [Collect diagnostic details](./troubleshooting.md#collect-diagnostic-details) to prepare a sanitized handoff for the team investigating the issue.

## Before you upgrade

Use the approved runbook and recovery plan for your version. Agree with the change lead on the time limit for a test offer so you have the same pass criterion before and after the upgrade.
