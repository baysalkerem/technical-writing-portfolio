# Integrate Nexus Connect

<PortfolioNotice />

Use this guide to connect a customer relationship management (CRM) service to Nexus Connect. Agents can then view the matching customer record while handling an interaction.

## Integration tasks

- [Connect a CRM integration](./crm-connection.md) to authorize access, map customer IDs, and test the connection.
- [Verify the integration](./crm-connection.md#verify-the-integration) with a test interaction. Confirm that the **Customer** panel shows the matching record and only the approved fields.
- [Troubleshoot the integration](./crm-connection.md#if-the-test-fails) if the connection fails, the record is missing, or the wrong fields appear.

## Before you connect a CRM

Confirm that you have the **Integration administrator** role and an approved integration account. Ask the CRM administrator for the approved field list and prepare a test record.
