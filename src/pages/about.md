## What I can help with

I help software and telecommunications teams turn complex technical information into clear, practical documentation. I write user guides, administrator procedures, and operational runbooks, and improve existing content for clarity, consistency, and ease of use.

The <Link to="/docs/case-studies/ai-assisted-documentation">AI-assisted documentation case study</Link> records the source checks and local tests used to review the infrastructure drafts, along with the validation still pending.

## Style guides and editorial standards

I follow the team’s style guide, terminology, and templates. If we keep running into the same editorial question, I write down what we decide so the next writer or reviewer can use it.

This portfolio includes versions based on the [Microsoft Writing Style Guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/welcome/) and the [Google developer documentation style guide](https://developers.google.com/style). Use the style switch to explore both. I’m also familiar with The Chicago Manual of Style.

## Tools and domains

I’ve documented Session Initiation Protocol (SIP), voice over IP (VoIP), communications hardware, customer relationship management (CRM) integrations, and software upgrades. My tools include DITA, Oxygen XML Editor, SDL Tridion Docs, Adobe Experience Manager, Confluence, and Jira.

I built this portfolio with Markdown, HTML, CSS, Git, and Docusaurus. It uses reusable components, validated YAML metadata, and GitHub Actions checks for Markdown, links, and production builds.

## Beyond technical writing

My background is in Russian translation and applied linguistics, including two years living in Nizhny Novgorod, Russia—a beautiful city with a name that took longer to pronounce than my degree. My experience spans sales, editorial project management, and technical writing at international companies. Working across countries and cultures is familiar territory, with Turkish, English, and Russian all part of the mix.

Earlier work includes translating and editing Russian literature for Turkish readers. You can browse the books in my [translation portfolio](https://baysalkerem.github.io/translation/en/).

Other interests include gaming and tinkering with app ideas of my own. Music gets a fair share of my time. I play bass, electric guitar, and occasionally drums, and experiment with music production.

Sometimes it’s nice to work on something you can turn up loud.
